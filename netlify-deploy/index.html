(03) — The Mod Cut Fit Guide

A garment should follow the body — never fight it.

“We don't fit women to our sizes.
We cut our sizes to women.”

— Head Cutter, Design Machine

The "mod cut" is our house philosophy: a modern, honest silhouette drafted from real measurements — not vanity sizing. Take fifteen minutes with a soft measuring tape, then compare against the three dimensions below. When in doubt, message us your numbers and a stylist will confirm your size before you check out.

01

The Bust

Measure around the fullest point, tape parallel to the floor, arms relaxed down. Do not pull tight — the mod cut needs one finger of ease.

Between two sizes?

Choose the size that fits your largest measurement — our first alteration is always free, in-store or by mail. For structured blazers, size up; for drape, stay true.
